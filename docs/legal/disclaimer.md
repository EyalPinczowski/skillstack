# Skillstack – Disclaimer

**Short version (shown under every generated plan and in the plan summary):**

> Suggestions only. Courses and exams are offered by other organizations – check prices and details on their sites before paying. AI-generated content may be inaccurate. Skillstack doesn't guarantee certificates, interviews or jobs.

**Full version (shown on the Terms page and in the plan builder's info panel):**

Skillstack suggests learning paths based on your answers and on a curated catalog of courses and certifications offered by third parties. We try to keep the catalog accurate, but prices, links, availability and certificate requirements change often and are controlled by each provider. Items marked "needs check" or "Unverified" haven't been confirmed. Some content in Skillstack – plans, day overviews, practice questions and feedback – is generated by AI and may be wrong or incomplete. Skillstack is a planning and practice tool: it doesn't guarantee that you'll pass an exam, earn a certificate, or get a job. Use your own judgment, and check anything important before you rely on it.
